> **This is a fork** of [lachholden/obsidian-recipe-view](https://github.com/lachholden/obsidian-recipe-view).
> It adds bilingual recipes, buttons to switch between the note and recipe view, and quantity
> fixes. See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), and [Installation](#installation) to install it with BRAT.

Store, write, and manage your recipes in as regular Obsidian notes in **portable, future-proof markdown**, then view them as a gorgeous **interactive recipe card** when it's time to cook in the kitchen.

**Features include:**
- 📒 Works with your recipes as-is, in whatever format you like to write them
- 🎨 Strives for maximum compatability with custom themes
- 🌈 Lets you use all the markdown that works in the rest of your vault
- ⚖️ Scales the quantities in your recipes easily
* ⚙️ Can split your recipes into two columns or separate ingredients out by step, for easier reference while cooking
- ✅ Makes ingredient lists cross-out-able
- 📌 Lets you highlight steps to keep track of where you're up to
- 🌍 Shows bilingual recipes one language at a time, with each ingredient's and step's translation a tap away
- 📱 Works on phones and tablets

**Why keep your recipes in Obsidian?**
- 🗃 Portable and future-proof markdown
- 📝 Everything is a note – keep your nicely formatted recipes in the same folders as your scanned magazine clippings
- 🌏 Cross-link and tag your recipes, link them to notes on technique, keep a baking log in your daily notes, or use Dataview or Kanban to plan out your cooking
- ☁️ Write them on your laptop, check ingredients in the store on your phone, and cook from them in the kitchen with your iPad

## Installation
This fork is installed with [BRAT](https://github.com/TfTHacker/obsidian42-brat), which installs plugins from GitHub and keeps them updated. It works on phones and tablets too.

1. In Obsidian, open **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**, then install and enable **BRAT**.
2. In BRAT's settings, choose **Add beta plugin**, enter `mavi-baykus/obsidian-recipe-view`, and choose **Add plugin**.
3. Turn on **Recipe view** under **Settings → Community plugins** if it isn't already on.

The fork uses the same plugin id as the original, so it replaces the original plugin if that's installed, and keeps its settings. BRAT checks for new versions when Obsidian starts (if *Auto-update plugins at startup* is on in BRAT's settings), or when you run the command *BRAT: Plugins: Check for updates to all beta plugins and UPDATE*. If Obsidian's own plugin updater ever offers an update for Recipe view, it's the original plugin; updating would replace the fork.

The original plugin is still available in the [Obsidian community plugins browser](obsidian://show-plugin?id=recipe-view).

## Documentation
To learn how to use the plugin, read the [documentation in the `docs` folder](docs/index.rst). The [original plugin's documentation](https://obsidian-recipe-view.readthedocs.io/) doesn't include this fork's changes.

## Additional settings
The [Style Settings](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-style-settings) plugin will let you configure aspects of how the recipe card appears.
